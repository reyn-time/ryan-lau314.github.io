Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy describes how a privately operated n8n automation application uses information authorized through Google OAuth. The application is intended for use by its operator and authorized collaborators, not as a publicly available service.

Information accessed

With authorization, the application may access Google Drive files and Google Sheets content needed to run configured workflows, such as retrieving media files and reading publishing schedules or content metadata. It also processes the account information and authorization tokens necessary to connect to Google services.

How information is used

Information is used to run the requested automation workflows, including preparing and publishing content to services configured by the operator. Google user data is not used to train generalized AI models or for advertising.

Sharing of information

Content selected for publication may be transmitted to configured publishing platforms, such as Facebook or Instagram. Data may also be processed by the infrastructure providers needed to operate n8n and its integrations. Google user data is not sold.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in the operator's private n8n environment. Workflow inputs, outputs, and execution logs may be retained to support operation and troubleshooting. Access to the automation environment is restricted to authorized users, and reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored information.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as needed for the configured workflows and their maintenance. The operator can delete stored workflow data and credentials. Google access can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, please open an issue in the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not include private information, credentials, or sensitive personal data in public issues.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's data practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date.
